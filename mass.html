THE HOLY MASS

What happens at Mass?

Mass is the central act of Catholic worship, centered on the Liturgy of the Word and the Liturgy of the Eucharist.

01

Introductory Rites

The congregation gathers, the priest enters, the Sign of the Cross is made, and the community prepares to worship.

02

Liturgy of the Word

Scripture is proclaimed and the homily helps apply God’s Word to life.

03

Liturgy of the Eucharist

Bread and wine are brought forward. Catholics believe they become the Body and Blood of Christ.

04

Communion Rite

The Church prays the Lord’s Prayer, shares a sign of peace, and receives Holy Communion according to Catholic practice.

05

Concluding Rites

The people are blessed and sent forth to live the Gospel.
